# Tribute to Sheikh Ashik Rahman

শেখ আশিক রহমান স্যারের জন্য একটি সম্মানজনক ওয়েবসাইট।

A premium tribute website for Sheikh Ashik Rahman — Lecturer at Kurigram City College and a beloved mathematics teacher. His journey, lessons and heartfelt messages, with a live countdown to his birthday and festive confetti.

## How to open

Just open `index.html` in a web browser.
